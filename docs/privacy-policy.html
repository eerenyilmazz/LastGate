2. Toplanan Veriler

LastGate, herhangi bir kişisel veri toplamaz, saklamaz veya üçüncü taraflarla paylaşmaz. Oyun içi tüm veriler (ilerleme, ayarlar) yalnızca cihazınızda yerel olarak saklanır.

ENGLISH

1. Overview

LastGate is a mobile strategy game developed by Code Vivus. This privacy policy explains how the application handles personal data.

2. Data Collected

LastGate does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All in-game data (progress, settings) is stored locally on your device only.

The following technical data may be collected anonymously:

  • Crash reports: When the app crashes, anonymous technical information is sent to Sentry for debugging purposes. This data contains no personally identifiable information.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Sentry — Crash monitoring. Privacy policy: sentry.io/privacy
  • Google AdMob — Ad service (optional). Google privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

4. Children's Privacy

The application is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from this age group.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

6. Contact

For privacy-related questions: codevivus@gmail.com
